Texas Caviar

6 servings

Prep: 15 mins

Ingredients

  • 400 g tin black beans, drained (240g, or ~ 1 1/4 cups, when drained)
  • 400 g tin black eyed beans, drained (240g, or ~ 1 1/4 cups, when drained)
  • 1 red pepper, diced
  • 3 small spring onions, diced (or 1 large)
  • 200 g tin sweetcorn, drained (160g, or ~ 1 cup, when drained)
  • 2 Tbsp pumpkin seeds

Steps

  1. Add all of the salad ingredients to a large mixing bowl
  2. If desired add roasted potatoes to make it more filing or a avocado olive oil dressing
