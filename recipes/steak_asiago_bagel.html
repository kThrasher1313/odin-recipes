Steak Asiago Bagel Sandwich

Ingredients

  • Asiago Bagel
  • Shaved Steak
  • Provolone Cheese
  • Red Onions
  • Roasted Red Bell Peppers
  • Arugula
  • Mayo
  • Salsa Verde

Steps

  1. Cook the steak in a pan over medium high heat with oil until browned
  2. Mix the mayo and salsa verde togetehr to make a spread
  3. Assemble Everything together
