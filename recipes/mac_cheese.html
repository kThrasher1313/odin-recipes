Mac and Cheese

6 servings

Ingredients

  • 7 1/4 oz Rotini Pasta
  • 1 lb ground chicken
  • 1/2 tsp organic garlic powder or minced garlic
  • 1/2 tsp organic onion powder or minced onions
  • salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 8 oz Cheddar Cheese Sauce
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1 1/4 cups 2% milk

Steps

  1. Cook pasta per package directions.
  2. While pasta is cooking, add chicken, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and salt to saute pan on MED-HIGH. Cook, stirring to break up pieces, about 5 min until cooked through. Set aside.
  3. If using minced onion/garlic add to pan while broccoli is cooking and chicken is set aside.
  4. Cook broccoli per package directions; set aside.
  5. Strain pasta; return to stockpot on LOW.
  6. Add cheese packets, sour cream, shredded cheddar, and milk to stockpot with pasta. Stir frequently to fully incorporate ingredients.
  7. Add warmed broccoli and cooked chicken; stir until combined and warmed through.
