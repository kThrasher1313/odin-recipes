Baked Chicken Nuggets and Potao Wedge

6 servings

Prep: 10 mins, Cook: 30-40 mins

Ingredients

  • 3 skinless, boneless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup Italian seasoned bread crumbs
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup butter, melted
  • 2 pounds red potatoes, about 6-8 potatoes
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon onion powder
  • pinch black pepper

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F
  2. Cut the potatoes into about 8 wedges (They should be less than 1/2 inch thick on the wide edge, if your potatoes are larger, cut any larger wedges in half again). Pat the potato wedges dry with paper towels to absorb excess starch.
  3. In a large bowl, combine potato wedges and oil, toss to coat. Stir together salt, paprika, garlic power, onion powder, and pepper. Sprinkle over potato wedges and toss to coat.
  4. Transfer potato wedges to your prepared baking sheet, arranging them in a single layer so they aren't overlapping, and doing your best to have them not touching. Bake for 15-20 minutes. Prepare chicken while cooking fro first 15 min. Remove from oven, turn over the potato wedges with fork or tongs. Return to oven for 15-20 minutes.
  5. Cut chicken breasts into 1 1/2-inch pieces.
  6. Mix the bread crumbs, Parmesan, basil, thyme, and salt together well in a medium bowl. Put melted butter in a bowl or dish for dipping.Dip chicken pieces into the melted butter first, then coat with the bread crumb mixture.
  7. Place the well-coated chicken nuggets on a lightly greased cookie sheet in a single layer.Bake nuggets in the preheated oven until browned and chicken is cooked through, about 20 minutes.
