Gravy

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp butter, bacon grease, or pan drippings from cooked meat
  • 4 tbsp all purpose flour
  • 2 cups of broth
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optionally worcestershire sauce or fresh herbs like thyme and rosemary

Steps

  1. Melt the fat/butter in a sauce pan over medium heat. Gradually whisk in the flour until smooth. Cook and stir for about 1 to 2 minutes.
  2. Very slowly add the broth while whisking aggressively.
  3. Bring the mixture to a low boil then reduce to medium low heat. Let simmer for 3 to 5 min stirring occasionally until it reaches desired thickness
