Italian Sausage Bowl

4 servings

40 min

Ingredients

  • 17 oz 5 links spicy pork sausage (cut into 3/4-inch pieces)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons yellow mustard
  • 1 teaspoon of lemon juice
  • salt and ground pepper
  • 1 large or 2 small head of broccoli, cut into small florets
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes cut in half
  • Optional: add a base like rice or roasted red potatoes

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450° F and line a sheet pan with foil or parchment paper.
  2. Place the sausage on the prepared baking sheet. Roast in the oven for 20 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, in a small bowl whisk together olive oil, yellow mustard, lemon juice and salt and pepper.
  4. Remove the sausage from the oven, flip them and add broccoli and cherry tomatoes.
  5. Pour the mustard mixture over the veggies and toss well to combine.
  6. Return the baking sheet to the oven and roast for more 15-20 minutes or until the sausage is cooked through and the veggies are tender.
  7. Divide roasted veggies and sausage between four 22oz glass meal prep containers. Store in the fridge for up to 4 days.
