Goat Cheese and Jam Sandwich

Ingredients

  • Goat cheese
  • Raspberry jam
  • Arugula
  • Balsamic glaze
  • Sliced ham
  • Salt
  • Sour dough

Steps

  1. Toast the sour dough and spread goat cheese directly on the bread to create a moisture barrier so it doesnt get soggy
  2. Add all other ingredients in the middle
