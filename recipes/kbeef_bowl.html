Korean Beef Bowl

5 servings

Ingredients

  • Green onions for garnish
  • Sesame seeds
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • Rice
  • Cooking oil
  • Spicy mayo
  • Avocado

For sauce

  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 tablespoon oyster sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ginger
  • red pepper flakes to taste
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch

For cucumber salad

  • Sliced cucumbers
  • Rice vinegar
  • Chili crisp oil
  • Soy sauce
  • Balsamic vinagrette

Steps

  1. Cook the rice in a rice cooker
  2. Brown ground beef in pan with oil over medium heat
  3. Drizzle sauce over ground beef in pan and bring to a boil.
  4. Lower heat and simmer for a few minutes
  5. Remove beef from heat and plate with rice, onions, spicy mayo, sesame seeds, and avocado
