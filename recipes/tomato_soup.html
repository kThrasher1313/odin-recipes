Creamy Tomato Basil Soup

9 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 large yellow onions, chopped
  • 1 jar (16oz) roasted red peppers drained and chopped
  • 3 tbsp jarred minced garlic
  • 10 cans (15oz each) fire-roasted crushed or whole peeled tomatoes
  • 12 cups low sodium vegetable broth
  • 3 cans (15 oz each) cannellini beans rinsed and drained
  • 2.5 blocks (30-35oz total) silken tofu
  • 4.5 cups nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 cups fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: red pepper flakes for heat

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large stockpot or dutch oven over medium heat. Add the chopped yellow onions and saute for about 6-8 minutes until soft and translucent.
  2. Add the garlic and drained roasted red peppers. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Stir in the dried oregano, salt, and black pepper. Pour in the cans of fire roasted tomatoes with their juices and the vegetable broth. Add the drained cannellini beans and slide in th eblocks of silken tofu.
  4. Bring the pot to a gentle boil, then reduce heat to medium low. Let it simmer for 12-15 minutes, allowing all the ingredients to heat through and the flavors to combine.
  5. Turn off the heat and stir in the fresh basil leaves. Use an immersion blender directly in the pot to blend the soup until completely smooth, creamy, and velvety.
  6. Let the soup cool for 5-10 mins so its warm, not boiling hot, then whisk in the Greek yogurt until smooth.
